Mode: Persuade. Brand-led motion, professional home-service clarity.
Confirmed direction from user's brief: vibrant purple/orange/cyan branding; supplied logo and van; animated but not cheesy.
World: a polished automotive brand campaign meets sunny Florida home comfort. Clean white and cool-gray canvas, deep aubergine stage, orange type accents, cyan service panels. Barlow Condensed display and Manrope text, locally hosted.

No invented reviews, ratings, client photos, statistics or certifications. No generic filler card grid. Keep forms with existing ServiceTitan.
