# Wowzer design

Mode: Persuade. Brand-led motion, professional home-service clarity.
Confirmed direction from user's brief: vibrant purple/orange/cyan branding; supplied logo and van; animated but not cheesy.
World: a polished automotive brand campaign meets sunny Florida home comfort. Warm cream canvas, deep aubergine stage, orange type accents, cyan service panels. Barlow Condensed display and Manrope text, locally hosted.
First viewport: clear navigation; giant editorial service promise left; supplied wrapped van over a slowly rotating geometric sun disc right; immediate scheduling and phone actions. Service labels anchor the scene.
Signature interaction: van arrival plus pointer depth and scroll drift; distinct airflow animation on HVAC and concentric water ripples on Plumbing. Slow banner motion, quiet reveals, arrow and image hover responses. Pause and reduced motion supported.
Page structure: opening brand scene, horizontal trust strip, two asymmetric service panels, honesty/process narrative, membership and financing feature, location list, native FAQ disclosures, large closing booking callout.
No invented reviews, ratings, client photos, statistics or certifications. No generic filler card grid. Keep forms with existing ServiceTitan.
